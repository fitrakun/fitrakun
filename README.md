# 💫 About Me:

# Hi there, I'm Fitra Rahmamuliani 👋 

### Technical Lead | HCI Researcher | Frontend Architect

I bridge the gap between rigorous **Human-Computer Interaction (HCI)** research and high-performance **Frontend Architecture**. With over 10+ years of experience and a Master of Engineering from Japan, I specialize in building scalable digital products that are as intuitive as they are technically sound.

---

### 🚀 Professional Highlights
* **Technical Lead @ Rolling Glory:** Leading and architecting multi-tenant React/TypeScript platforms, specializing in dynamic theme-based engines for enterprise clients.
* **Best Instructor @ RevoU:** Mentoring the next generation of engineers in JavaScript, React, and Frontend Deployment (MSIB, Master Class, Coding Camp programs).
* **HCI Researcher:** Sakuma Award recipient at Kochi University of Technology. Published researcher in intergenerational fitness tracking and user-centered design.
* **Co-Founder @ Fierofea Books:** Leading a team of 30+ volunteers to increase literacy access across Indonesia.

---

### 🛠️ Tech Stack & Expertise
* **Languages:** TypeScript, JavaScript (ES6+), Python, HTML5, SCSS/SASS
* **Frameworks:** React.js, Next.js, Vue.js, Nuxt.js (3.x, 2.x), Laravel
* **Architecture:** Multi-tenant Systems, Design Systems (Mantine/Tailwind), Micro-frontends, PWA
* **Design & Research:** User-Centered Design (UCD), Qualitative Research, Figma, Adobe Experience Manager (AEM)
